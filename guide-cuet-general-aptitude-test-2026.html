CUET (UG) General Aptitude Test: syllabus, pace and a study plan

The General Aptitude Test is one of the 37 CUET (UG) subjects. Here is what the official bulletin lists, and what that means for your preparation.

Key insights

  • 50 compulsory questions in 60 minutes: 72 seconds per question.
  • Marking is +5 / −1, so a guess pays once you are more than 1 in 6 sure.
  • The syllabus mixes general knowledge and current affairs with mental ability, numerical ability and reasoning.
  • Quant in the GAT is basic: arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration and statistics.

The official syllabus

The CUET (UG) 2026 bulletin lists the General Aptitude Test syllabus as: general knowledge, current affairs, general mental ability, numerical ability, quantitative reasoning (simple use of basic maths: arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, statistics) and logical and analytical reasoning.

The pace

60 minutes for 50 questions is 72 seconds each. GK questions take 10–20 seconds if you know them. Use the time you save on the reasoning and quant questions.

FAQ

Is the GAT compulsory?

No. It is one of the subjects you may choose, up to five in total. Check whether your target course requires it.

Is there negative marking?

Yes, −1 for a wrong answer and +5 for a correct one, according to the 2026 bulletin.
