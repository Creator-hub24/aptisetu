Corrections & editorial policy

How our questions are made, how to report a mistake, and a public record of what we fixed.

How our questions are made

Every question, passage and data set on AptiSetu was written by our team. None are copied from past papers, coaching material or other websites. Each question has a worked solution, and our build process checks the stored answers automatically before anything is published. We are an independent site, not affiliated with NTA, the IIMs, IBPS, SBI, SSC or any exam body.

Found a mistake?

Every question on a quiz page, a sectional or a practice set has a ⚑ Report an error link. It opens an email with the question ID and page already filled in, so you only need to say what looks wrong. You can also write to us from the Contact page. A wrong answer key, an unclear question, a typo or a broken chart are all worth reporting.

What we do with a report

  • We check the question and its solution again.
  • If it is wrong, we fix it and add an entry to the log below, with the date and what changed.
  • If it is correct, we will explain why where we can, and improve the solution if it was unclear.

Practice questions are written by people and can contain mistakes. If you are preparing for an exam, please treat our solutions as a study aid and cross-check anything that surprises you.
