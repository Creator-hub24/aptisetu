Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about how the site works.

Is it free?

Yes. All notes, formula sheets and practice questions are free.

Do I need to sign up or log in?

Login only to save progress and unlock levels. Notes, quizzes, sectionals and Easy Quant set 1 of the CAT practice sets work without an account. Login uses a one-time link sent to your email; there is no password.

How do the CAT practice sets work?

Each CAT section has 15 sets of 10 questions: Easy (1–5), Medium (6–10) and Hard (11–15), with a 20-minute timer (25 for DILR). A level unlocks when you have finished every ready set in the level before it. Sets we are still writing show Coming soon.

Which exams does it cover?

Three tracks: CAT (VARC, DILR, QA), CUET (UG) (General Aptitude Test, English, Mathematics), and Bank / SSC / Placements (Quant, Reasoning, DILR).

How does timed mode work in the Bank / SSC quizzes?

Choose a section, an optional topic, the number of questions and the time per question (60, 90 or 120 seconds). The total time is shown as a countdown. You can move between questions, mark them for review, and see the score and worked solutions after you submit. CAT-style +3 / −1 marking is optional.

How do CAT sectionals work?

A CAT sectional is one section with a 40-minute countdown that submits automatically when time runs out. Marking is +3 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong MCQ, and 0 for a wrong TITA or a blank. RC passages and DILR sets stay together.

How do CUET sectionals work?

Up to 50 questions at the real pace of 72 seconds per question, with +5 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one. Where our bank has fewer than 50 questions, the sectional is shorter but keeps the same pace.

What is practice mode?

On the CAT and CUET tracks, practice mode has no countdown. You pick a topic, check each answer and see the full solution straight away.

Are these real exam questions?

No. All questions were written by us for practice, in the style of each exam. They are not taken from past papers.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, but only in your own browser. The site remembers an unfinished quiz so you can resume it, your daily practice streak, and your accuracy for each topic. This is stored in your browser's local storage on this device; it is never sent to us, and it won't follow you to another phone or browser. To delete it, use Reset my progress on the Practice page, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

What is Quick start?

On the Practice page, Quick start gives you 10 mixed Quant, Reasoning and DILR questions with 60 seconds each, in one tap.

Are you linked to NTA or the IIMs?

No. This is an independent site, not affiliated with NTA, the IIMs or any exam body. Always check official exam websites for dates and rules.

I found a mistake. What should I do?

Please contact us with the page and the question text.
