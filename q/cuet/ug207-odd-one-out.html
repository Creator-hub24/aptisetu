Which letter group is the odd one out? BDF, HJL, NPR, TVW
ANPR
BBDF
CHJL
DTVW
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: D. TVW

Solution: In BDF, HJL and NPR each letter is two places after the previous one (B+2 = D, D+2 = F). In TVW, V + 1 = W breaks the pattern (it should be TVX).
