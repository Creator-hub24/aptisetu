On which date did the Constitution of India come into force?
A26 January 1950
B26 November 1949
C15 August 1947
D26 January 1949
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. 26 January 1950

Solution: The Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution on 26 November 1949, and it came into force on 26 January 1950, which is celebrated as Republic Day.
