CUET (UG) General Aptitude Test: Time, Speed & Distance · UG14

MCQ · Easy · Quantitative Reasoning

A speed of 72 km/h is equal to:
A20 m/s
B25 m/s
C72 m/s
D12 m/s
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. 20 m/s

Solution: Multiply km/h by 5/18 to get m/s: 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s.
