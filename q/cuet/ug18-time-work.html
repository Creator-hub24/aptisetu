MCQ · Medium · Quantitative Reasoning

A and B together can finish a job in 12 days. A alone can finish it in 20 days. How long would B alone take?
A30 days
B8 days
C32 days
D24 days
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. 30 days

Solution: B's rate = 1/12 − 1/20 = 5/60 − 3/60 = 2/60 = 1/30, so B alone takes 30 days.
