MCQ · Hard

Job offers made by five companies this season
Company A80
Company B120
Company C60
Company D100
Company E40
If Company A's offers rise by 25% and Company C's rise by 50%, what percentage of the new total do these two companies make together?
A40.0%
B42.2%
C44.4%
D46.7%
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: B. 42.2%

Solution: A becomes 100 (+20) and C becomes 90 (+30). New total = 400 + 50 = 450. A + C = 190. 190/450 = 42.2%.
