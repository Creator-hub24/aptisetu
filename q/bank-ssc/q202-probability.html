Two cards are drawn at random, without replacement, from a well-shuffled pack of 52 cards. What is the probability that both are hearts?
A1/4
B1/16
C1/17
D13/102
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: C. 1/17

Solution in:
Solution: Ways to pick 2 hearts = C(13, 2) = 78; ways to pick any 2 cards = C(52, 2) = 1326. Probability = 78/1326 = 1/17.2 पान (hearts) चुनने के तरीके = C(13, 2) = 78; कोई भी 2 पत्ते चुनने के तरीके = C(52, 2) = 1326. प्रायिकता = 78/1326 = 1/17.
