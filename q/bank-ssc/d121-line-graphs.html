In which year did B's exports fall compared with the previous year?
A2024
B2022
C2023
D2021
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: D. 2021

Solution in:
Solution: B: 50 → 45 from 2020 to 2021 is the only fall; every later year rises (54, 63, 70, 84).B: 2020 से 2021 में 50 → 45 ही एकमात्र गिरावट है; बाद के सभी वर्षों में वृद्धि है (54, 63, 70, 84)।
