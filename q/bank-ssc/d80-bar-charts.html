MCQ · Easy

Job offers made by five companies this season
Company A80
Company B120
Company C60
Company D100
Company E40
Company B's offers are what percentage of the total?
A30%
B28%
C25%
D32%
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. 30%

Solution in:
Solution: Company B made 120 of the 400 offers in total. The percentage is 120/400 × 100 = 30%.कुल 400 ऑफ़र में से 120 कंपनी B ने दिए। प्रतिशत 120/400 × 100 = 30% है।
