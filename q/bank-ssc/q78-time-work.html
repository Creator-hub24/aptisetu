A and B together can finish a job in 12 days. A alone can finish it in 20 days. In how many days can B alone finish it?
A28 days
B32 days
C30 days
D36 days
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: C. 30 days

Solution in:
Solution: B's daily work = 1/12 − 1/20 = 5/60 − 3/60 = 2/60 = 1/30, so B alone takes 30 days.B का एक दिन का काम = 1/12 − 1/20 = 5/60 − 3/60 = 2/60 = 1/30, अतः B अकेले 30 दिन लेता है।
