At what rate of compound interest per annum will ₹2,500 amount to ₹3,025 in 2 years?
A12%
B11%
C10%
D10.5%
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: C. 10%

Solution in:
Solution: (1 + r/100)² = 3,025 / 2,500 = 1.21 = 1.1², so 1 + r/100 = 1.1 and r = 10%.(1 + r/100)² = 3,025 / 2,500 = 1.21 = 1.1², इसलिए 1 + r/100 = 1.1 और r = 10%.
