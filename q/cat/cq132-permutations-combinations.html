CAT Quantitative Ability: Permutations & Combinations · CQ132

TITA · Medium

In how many ways can 10 identical chocolates be distributed among 3 children so that each child gets at least 1 and at most 5?

Type in the answer (TITA): no options.

Show answer & solution

Correct answer: 18

Solution: Give each child 1 first: now a + b + c = 7 with each between 0 and 4. Without the upper limit: C(9, 2) = 36. Subtract the cases where one child gets 5 or more: set that child's share to 5 + a', leaving 2 to share: C(4, 2) = 6 ways, for each of 3 children = 18. Two children cannot both exceed. Answer = 36 − 18 = 18.
