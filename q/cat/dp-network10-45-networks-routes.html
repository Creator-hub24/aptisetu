A drone delivery network has seven pads. The table lists every permitted direct hop and its flight time in minutes (hops work in both directions); no other hops are permitted.

LinkTime
Pad A – Pad B4
Pad A – Pad C18
Pad A – Pad F9
Pad B – Pad C15
Pad B – Pad E6
Pad C – Pad D10
Pad C – Pad F12
Pad D – Pad E6
Pad D – Pad F9
Pad E – Pad G10

What is the smallest number of intermediate points on any route from Pad C to Pad D?

Type in the answer (TITA): no options.

Show answer & solution

Correct answer: 0

Solution: Step 1: check for a direct link. There is one, so 0 intermediate points are needed. Answer: 0.
