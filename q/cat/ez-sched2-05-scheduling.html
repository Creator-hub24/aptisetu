Scheduling

A clinic has five doctors, Uma, Varun, Wasim, Yamini and Zubin, each on duty on exactly one of five consecutive days, Day 1 to Day 5, one doctor per day. Each doctor works in a different ward: Wards A, B, C, D and E.

The following is known:

  • Varun is immediately before Uma.
  • Zubin's ward is Ward A.
  • Wasim is not on Day 2.
  • Wasim is not on Day 1.
  • The ward on Day 5 is Ward A.
  • Wasim's ward is Ward E.
  • The ward on Day 3 is Ward C.
  • Uma's ward is Ward D.
What is the ward of the doctor on Day 4?
AWard B
BWard E
CWard D
DWard A
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: B. Ward E

Solution: Step 1: use "Zubin's ward is Ward A." — this fixes: Zubin's ward is A. Step 2: use "The ward on Day 5 is Ward A." — this fixes: Zubin is on Day 5. Step 3: use "Wasim's ward is Ward E." — this fixes: Wasim's ward is E. Step 4: use "Uma's ward is Ward D." — this fixes: Uma's ward is D. Step 5: use "Varun is immediately before Uma." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 6: use "Wasim is not on Day 2." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 7: use "Wasim is not on Day 1." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 8: use "The ward on Day 3 is Ward C." — this fixes: Varun's ward is B; Yamini's ward is C; Uma is on Day 2; Varun is on Day 1; Wasim is on Day 4; Yamini is on Day 3. Final arrangement: Day 1: Varun (B); Day 2: Uma (D); Day 3: Yamini (C); Day 4: Wasim (E); Day 5: Zubin (A). Reading off: Ward E.
