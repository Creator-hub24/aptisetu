CAT Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension: Odd sentence out · EZV-17

TITA · Easy

Five jumbled sentences related to a topic are given below. Four of them can be put together to form a coherent paragraph. Identify the odd one out and key in its number.

  1. Sleep helps the brain store what we learned during the day.
  2. In experiments, people who slept after studying remembered more than those who stayed awake.
  3. Some animals, such as dolphins, sleep with one half of the brain at a time.
  4. This is why teachers warn against staying up all night before an exam.
  5. A rested brain recalls information more easily than a tired one.

Type in the answer (TITA): no options.

Show answer & solution

Correct answer: 3

Solution: Sentence 3 is the odd one out. The paragraph is about sleep and memory for study. Dolphin sleep is an unrelated fact.
