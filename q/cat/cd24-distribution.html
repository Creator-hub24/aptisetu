CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Distribution · CD24

MCQ · Hard

Data set: Sharing chocolates
Sharing chocolates

24 chocolates were shared among five friends P, Q, R, S and T. Each got at least one, and no two got the same number.

  • Q got twice as many as R.
  • S got exactly one more than T.
  • P got as many as Q and T together, and P got the most.
  • R got more than S.
Which two friends together got exactly half of all the chocolates?
AQ and R
BP and S
CP and T
DR and S
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. Q and R

Solution: Let T = t, so S = t + 1. Let R = r, so Q = 2r and P = 2r + t. Total: (2r + t) + 2r + r + (t + 1) + t = 5r + 3t + 1 = 24 ⇒ 5r + 3t = 23. Options: (r, t) = (4, 1) or (1, 6). R > S rules out (1, 6) (R = 1 < S = 7). So P = 9, Q = 8, R = 4, S = 2, T = 1. Q + R = 8 + 4 = 12. The other pairs: P + S = 11, P + T = 10, R + S = 6.
