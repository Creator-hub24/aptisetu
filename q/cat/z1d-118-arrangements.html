CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Arrangements · Z1D-118

MCQ · Hard

Data set: Interview week (Hard)
Interview week

Six interviews of candidates Aarav, Barkha, Cyrus, Damini, Eshwar and Fatima take place on six consecutive days, Monday to Saturday, one per day. Each candidate applies for a different role, analyst, designer, tester, manager, writer and trainer, and each comes from a different city, Nagpur, Surat, Ranchi, Kochi, Jammu and Patna.

The following is known:

  • The candidate for writer is also the candidate from Kochi.
  • Cyrus is scheduled on either the first or the last day.
  • Aarav is scheduled on either the first or the last day.
  • Aarav applies for trainer.
  • There is exactly one day between the days of the candidate for manager and Fatima.
  • The candidate from Ranchi and the candidate for tester are scheduled on consecutive days.
  • The candidate from Nagpur is scheduled on either the first or the last day.
  • The candidate from Ranchi is scheduled on some day before the candidate from Surat.
  • Damini is scheduled on some day before the candidate for tester.
  • There is exactly one day between the days of the candidate for analyst and Barkha.
  • The candidate for designer is scheduled on the day immediately before the candidate for manager.
  • The candidate for analyst is also the candidate from Jammu.
  • Aarav and the candidate from Kochi are scheduled on consecutive days.
  • Barkha and Damini are scheduled on consecutive days.
  • Either the candidate for tester is scheduled on Thursday or Barkha is scheduled on Monday, but not both.
Which of the following statements is true?
ABarkha and Cyrus are scheduled on consecutive days
BDamini and Cyrus are scheduled on consecutive days
CCyrus comes from Ranchi
DFatima is scheduled on Tuesday
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: B. Damini and Cyrus are scheduled on consecutive days

Solution: Step 1: “The candidate for writer is also the candidate from Kochi.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 2: “Aarav applies for trainer.” Now fixed: Aarav: trainer. Step 3: “The candidate for analyst is also the candidate from Jammu.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 4: “The candidate for designer is scheduled on the day immediately before the candidate for manager.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 5: “There is exactly one day between the days of the candidate for manager and Fatima.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 6: “There is exactly one day between the days of the candidate for analyst and Barkha.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 7: “Either the candidate for tester is scheduled on Thursday or Barkha is scheduled on Monday, but not both.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 8: “Cyrus is scheduled on either the first or the last day.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 9: “Aarav is scheduled on either the first or the last day.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 10: “The candidate from Nagpur is scheduled on either the first or the last day.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 11: “The candidate from Ranchi and the candidate for tester are scheduled on consecutive days.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 12: “Aarav and the candidate from Kochi are scheduled on consecutive days.” Now fixed: Barkha: Ranchi. Step 13: “Barkha and Damini are scheduled on consecutive days.” Now fixed: Aarav on Saturday; Barkha on Wednesday; Cyrus on Monday; Fatima on Friday; Barkha: manager; Cyrus: analyst; Fatima: writer; Aarav: Nagpur; Cyrus: Jammu; Fatima: Kochi. Step 14: “The candidate from Ranchi is scheduled on some day before the candidate from Surat.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 15: “Damini is scheduled on some day before the candidate for tester.” Now fixed: Damini on Tuesday; Eshwar on Thursday; Damini: designer; Eshwar: tester; Damini: Patna; Eshwar: Surat. Every possible arrangement was checked by computer and exactly one fits all the clues: Monday: Cyrus (analyst, Jammu); Tuesday: Damini (designer, Patna); Wednesday: Barkha (manager, Ranchi); Thursday: Eshwar (tester, Surat); Friday: Fatima (writer, Kochi); Saturday: Aarav (trainer, Nagpur). Checking each option against this arrangement, only “Damini and Cyrus are scheduled on consecutive days” holds.
