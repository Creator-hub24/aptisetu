Scheduling

Five trainers, Pooja, Qadir, Ritu, Sahil and Tara, each run one workshop on a different day of a week, Day 1 (Monday) to Day 5 (Friday). Each workshop is on a different subject: Excel, design, sales, writing and coding.

The following is known:

  • The subject on Day 3 is coding.
  • Tara is not on Day 1.
  • Pooja's subject is Excel.
  • Tara is immediately before Pooja.
  • Sahil is immediately before Qadir.
  • The subject on Day 4 is writing.
  • Ritu's subject is design.
What is Ritu's subject?
Acoding
BExcel
Cwriting
Ddesign
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: D. design

Solution: Step 1: use "Pooja's subject is Excel." — this fixes: Pooja's subject is Excel. Step 2: use "Ritu's subject is design." — this fixes: Ritu's subject is design. Step 3: use "The subject on Day 3 is coding." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 4: use "Tara is not on Day 1." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 5: use "Tara is immediately before Pooja." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 6: use "The subject on Day 4 is writing." — this fixes: Tara's subject is writing; Pooja is on Day 5; Tara is on Day 4. Step 7: use "Sahil is immediately before Qadir." — this fixes: Qadir's subject is coding; Sahil's subject is sales; Qadir is on Day 3; Ritu is on Day 1; Sahil is on Day 2. Final arrangement: Day 1: Ritu (design); Day 2: Sahil (sales); Day 3: Qadir (coding); Day 4: Tara (writing); Day 5: Pooja (Excel). Reading off: design.
