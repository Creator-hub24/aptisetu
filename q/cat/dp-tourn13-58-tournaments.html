CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Tournaments · DP-TOURN13-58

MCQ · Medium

Data set: Tournament (Medium)
Tournament

Five college quiz houses — Amber, Indigo, Jade, Ochre, Slate — met each other exactly once in a round-robin buzzer league. A win earned 3 points, a tie 2 points and a loss 0.

The following is known:

  • Jade won exactly two matches.
  • Slate drew exactly two matches.
  • Ochre beat Jade.
  • Indigo drew exactly one match.
  • Slate beat Indigo.
  • Amber finished with 0 points.
Which team finished with the most points?
ASlate
BIndigo
CTwo or more teams tied
DAmber
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: C. Two or more teams tied

Solution: Step 1: use "Amber finished with 0 points." — this fixes: Indigo beat Amber; Jade beat Amber; Ochre beat Amber; Slate beat Amber; Amber has 0 points. Step 2: use "Ochre beat Jade." — this fixes: Ochre beat Jade. Step 3: use "Slate beat Indigo." — this fixes: Slate beat Indigo. Step 4: use "Slate drew exactly two matches." — this fixes: Jade drew with Slate; Ochre drew with Slate; Slate has 10 points. Step 5: use "Jade won exactly two matches." — this fixes: Jade beat Indigo; Jade has 8 points. Step 6: use "Indigo drew exactly one match." — this fixes: Indigo drew with Ochre; Indigo has 5 points; Ochre has 10 points. Final arrangement: Amber v Indigo: Indigo; Amber v Jade: Jade; Amber v Ochre: Ochre; Amber v Slate: Slate; Indigo v Jade: Jade; Indigo v Ochre: draw; Indigo v Slate: Slate; Jade v Ochre: Ochre; Jade v Slate: draw; Ochre v Slate: draw; points Amber 0, Indigo 5, Jade 8, Ochre 10, Slate 10. Reading off: Two or more teams tied.
