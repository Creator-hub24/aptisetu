MCQ · Medium

A right circular cylinder of height 12 cm is placed inside a sphere of radius 10 cm so that both circular rims touch the sphere. What is the volume of the cylinder?
A576π cm³
B864π cm³
C768π cm³
D960π cm³
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: C. 768π cm³

Solution: The centre of the sphere is at mid-height, 6 cm from each rim. Rim radius r satisfies r² + 6² = 10², so r² = 64. Volume = π × 64 × 12 = 768π cm³.
