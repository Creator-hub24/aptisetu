Data set: Workshop renovation (Hard)
Workshop renovation

A renovation has eight tasks, A to H. The table gives each task's duration in days and the tasks that must be fully finished before it can start. A task, once started, is done by one worker without a break; a worker does one task at a time; any worker can do any task.

TaskDuration (days)Must follow
A3—
B4—
C2A
D5A, B
E3C
F4D, E
G2B
H5G
What is the smallest number of workers with which the renovation can still be finished in the minimum possible time found with unlimited workers?
A2
B3
C4
D5
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: B. 3

Solution: With enough workers, every task starts as soon as its predecessors finish. Finish days: A 3, B 4, C 5, D 9 (after A and B), E 8, F 13 (after D at 9 and E at 8), G 6, H 11. So the project needs 13 days, along the chain B → D → F (4 + 5 + 4). Working backwards from day 13, the latest finishes are A 4, B 4, C 6, D 9, E 9, F 13, G 8, H 13, so the slacks are A 1, B 0, C 1, D 0, E 1, F 0, G 2, H 2. Two workers need at least 28/2 = 14 days, so 13 days needs at least 3. With three it works: start every task as early as possible — at most three tasks (C, D and G, during day 4 to 5) are ever in progress together, so 3 workers suffice.
