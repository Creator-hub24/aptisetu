CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Arrangements · Z1D-039

MCQ · Medium

Data set: Six storeys (Medium)
Six storeys

Six tenants, Gauri, Harish, Ira, Jatin, Kamala and Lokesh, live in a six-storey building, one per floor (floor 1 is the lowest). Each owns a different vehicle, scooter, sedan, bicycle, jeep, van and motorbike, and each works in a different field, nurse, chef, pilot, lawyer, painter and teacher.

The following is known:

  • The nurse lives on the floor immediately below Gauri.
  • Lokesh and the lawyer live on adjacent floors.
  • There are exactly 2 floors between the floors of the bicycle owner and the teacher.
  • The painter lives on floor 4.
  • Harish owns the scooter.
  • Lokesh owns the jeep.
  • The teacher lives on floor 1.
  • The pilot is also the sedan owner.
  • The sedan owner lives on either the top floor or the bottom floor.
  • There are exactly 3 floors between the floors of Kamala and the nurse.
  • The motorbike owner lives on floor 1.
  • The scooter owner lives on the floor immediately below Jatin.
Which of the following statements is true?
ALokesh lives on floor 6
BHarish is the painter
CIra lives on floor 1
DIra and Gauri live on adjacent floors
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: D. Ira and Gauri live on adjacent floors

Solution: Step 1: “The painter lives on floor 4.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 2: “The teacher lives on floor 1.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 3: “The motorbike owner lives on floor 1.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 4: “Harish owns the scooter.” Now fixed: Harish: scooter. Step 5: “Lokesh owns the jeep.” Now fixed: Lokesh: jeep. Step 6: “The pilot is also the sedan owner.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 7: “The nurse lives on the floor immediately below Gauri.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 8: “The scooter owner lives on the floor immediately below Jatin.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 9: “There are exactly 2 floors between the floors of the bicycle owner and the teacher.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 10: “There are exactly 3 floors between the floors of Kamala and the nurse.” Now fixed: Gauri on floor 6; Kamala on floor 1; Kamala: motorbike; Kamala: teacher. Step 11: “The sedan owner lives on either the top floor or the bottom floor.” Now fixed: Gauri: sedan; Gauri: pilot. Step 12: “Lokesh and the lawyer live on adjacent floors.” Now fixed: Harish on floor 3; Ira on floor 5; Jatin on floor 4; Lokesh on floor 2; Ira: van; Jatin: bicycle; Harish: lawyer; Ira: nurse; Jatin: painter; Lokesh: chef. Every possible arrangement was checked by computer and exactly one fits all the clues: floor 1: Kamala (motorbike, teacher); floor 2: Lokesh (jeep, chef); floor 3: Harish (scooter, lawyer); floor 4: Jatin (bicycle, painter); floor 5: Ira (van, nurse); floor 6: Gauri (sedan, pilot). Checking each option against this arrangement, only “Ira and Gauri live on adjacent floors” holds.
