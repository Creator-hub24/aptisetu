CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Games with rules · B12D-20

MCQ · Hard

Data set: Counting-out game (Medium)
Counting-out game

Seven children — Anu, Bala, Chetan, Dev, Esha, Farah and Gopal — stand in a circle in that order, clockwise (Gopal is next to Anu). They choose a whole number m from 2 to 9. Starting with Anu as 1, they count clockwise round the circle, and the child who is counted m leaves. Counting starts again at 1 from the next child still in the circle, and so on, until one child is left.

  • Anu was not the first child to leave.
  • Gopal was not among the first three children to leave.
  • Dev was still in the circle when Anu left.
If the children had used a number one larger than m, with everything else the same, who would have been left at the end?
ABala
BChetan
CDev
DGopal
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. Bala

Solution: Play the game for each m (leaving order, last name = the child left in the circle): m = 2: Bala, Dev, Farah, Anu, Esha, Chetan, Gopal; m = 3: Chetan, Farah, Bala, Gopal, Esha, Anu, Dev; m = 4: Dev, Anu, Farah, Esha, Gopal, Chetan, Bala; m = 5: Esha, Chetan, Bala, Dev, Gopal, Anu, Farah; m = 6: Farah, Esha, Gopal, Bala, Anu, Dev, Chetan; m = 7: Gopal, Anu, Chetan, Farah, Bala, Dev, Esha; m = 8: Anu, Chetan, Farah, Esha, Bala, Gopal, Dev; m = 9: Bala, Esha, Chetan, Dev, Anu, Farah, Gopal. Anu leaves first only for m = 8. Gopal is among the first three for m = 6 (third) and m = 7 (first). Dev has already left when Anu leaves for m = 2, 4, 5 and 9. Only m = 3 satisfies all three clues. With m = 3 the children leave in the order Chetan, Farah, Bala, Gopal, Esha, Anu, and Dev is left. With 4 the order is Dev, Anu, Farah, Esha, Gopal, Chetan, Bala, so Bala is left.
