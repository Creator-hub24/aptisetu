CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Tables with missing data · EZ-TAB3-03

TITA · Easy

Data set: Table with missing data (Easy)
Table with missing data

The table shows the stock (in tonnes) received by a warehouse for five goods over three months. Some entries are missing; the row totals and column totals are complete.

JanFebMarTotal
Rice376?60
Wheat59?37
Pulses2822555
Sugar?221045
Oil4?1830
Total876773227

All values are whole numbers; units: tonnes.

By how much did Oil change from Jan to Mar? Enter the increase (use a minus sign for a decrease).

Type in the answer (TITA): no options.

Show answer & solution

Correct answer: 14

Solution: Step 1: fill the blanks from the row totals (each row has one blank): Rice, Mar = row total 60 − (37 + 6) = 17; Wheat, Mar = row total 37 − (5 + 9) = 23; Sugar, Jan = row total 45 − (22 + 10) = 13; Oil, Feb = row total 30 − (4 + 18) = 8. Step 2: check against the column totals: Jan = 87, Feb = 67, Mar = 73 (all match). Step 3: subtract the first period from the last. Answer: 14.
