CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Arrangements · EZ-ROW2-02

MCQ · Easy

Data set: Arrangement (Easy)
Arrangement

Five students, Farhan, Gita, Harsh, Isha and Jai, stand in a queue at positions 1 to 5 (position 1 is the front). Each holds a ticket for a different city: Pune, Kochi, Agra, Surat and Patna.

The following is known:

  • Jai is not at position 3.
  • Harsh's city is Pune.
  • Gita is not at position 3.
  • The city at position 3 is Kochi.
  • Farhan is not at position 5.
  • The city at position 1 is Patna.
  • Isha is immediately before Gita.
  • Farhan's city is Agra.
What is Farhan's city?
APatna
BPune
CAgra
DKochi
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: C. Agra

Solution: Step 1: use "Harsh's city is Pune." — this fixes: Harsh's city is Pune. Step 2: use "Farhan's city is Agra." — this fixes: Farhan's city is Agra. Step 3: use "Jai is not at position 3." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 4: use "Gita is not at position 3." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 5: use "The city at position 3 is Kochi." — this fixes: Isha's city is Kochi; Isha is at position 3. Step 6: use "Isha is immediately before Gita." — this fixes: Gita is at position 4. Step 7: use "The city at position 1 is Patna." — this fixes: Gita's city is Surat; Jai's city is Patna; Jai is at position 1. Step 8: use "Farhan is not at position 5." — this fixes: Farhan is at position 2; Harsh is at position 5. Final arrangement: position 1: Jai (Patna); position 2: Farhan (Agra); position 3: Isha (Kochi); position 4: Gita (Surat); position 5: Harsh (Pune). Reading off: Agra.
