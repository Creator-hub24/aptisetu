Data set: Two facing rows (Easy)
Two facing rows

Six friends — Amit, Bhavna, Charu, Deepak, Ela and Firoz — sit in two rows of three seats. The north row faces south and the south row faces north, so each person directly faces one person in the other row.

  • Amit sits in the north row, in an end seat, and faces Ela.
  • Firoz sits immediately to Amit's right.
  • Charu sits in the middle seat of a row.
  • Deepak does not sit in the same row as Amit.
  • Bhavna does not face Firoz.
Who sits immediately to Charu's left?
AEla
BDeepak
CFiroz
DNo one
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: B. Deepak

Solution: Firoz is immediately to Amit's right, in the same row, and Amit is at an end, so Firoz is in the north row's middle seat. Charu must then take the middle seat of the south row, facing Firoz. Deepak is in the south row, so the north row's remaining end seat goes to Bhavna. Ela faces Amit, so Deepak takes the other south end seat, facing Bhavna. Directions: the north row faces south, so a north-row person's right is towards the west. With Firoz on Amit's right, Amit is at the east end: north row (west to east) Bhavna, Firoz, Amit; south row (west to east) Deepak, Charu, Ela. Charu faces north, so Charu's left is towards the west, where Deepak sits.
