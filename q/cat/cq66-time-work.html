CAT Quantitative Ability: Time & Work · CQ66

MCQ · Easy

A can finish a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. They work together for 4 days, after which A leaves. In how many days in all is the job completed?
A10
B11
C12
D14
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: C. 12

Solution: In 4 days together: 4 × (1/12 + 1/18) = 4 × 5/36 = 5/9 of the work. The remaining 4/9 is done by B at 1/18 per day, which takes 8 days. Total = 4 + 8 = 12 days.
