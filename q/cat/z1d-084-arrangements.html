Data set: Family floors (Hard)
Family floors

Six families, the Bhatts, the Chawlas, the Dases, the Gills, the Iyers and the Joshis, live on floors 1 to 6 of a building, one family per floor (floor 1 is the lowest). Each family owns a different car colour, silver, maroon, beige, navy, olive and teal, and each has a different number of members, two, three, four, five, six and seven.

The following is known:

  • The family with the teal car lives on either the top floor or the bottom floor.
  • The Iyers lives on either the top floor or the bottom floor.
  • There is exactly one floor between the floors of the family of four and the family with the silver car.
  • The Gills are a family of six.
  • The Dases lives on the floor immediately below the Chawlas.
  • Either the Gills lives on floor 4 or the family with the silver car lives on floor 2, but not both.
  • There are exactly 2 floors between the floors of the Gills and the family with the silver car.
  • The family with the maroon car and the family of seven live on adjacent floors.
  • There is exactly one floor between the floors of the family of three and the Gills.
  • The family with the maroon car lives on the floor immediately below the family with the olive car.
  • The family of three is also the family with the beige car.
  • The Chawlas lives on some floor below the family of two.
  • Either the Joshis lives on floor 6 or the family with the maroon car lives on floor 5, but not both.
Who is on the floor immediately above the Gills?
Athe Bhatts
Bthe Iyers
Cthe Joshis
Dthe Chawlas
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. the Bhatts

Solution: Step 1: “The Gills are a family of six.” Now fixed: the Gills: six. Step 2: “The family of three is also the family with the beige car.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 3: “The Dases lives on the floor immediately below the Chawlas.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 4: “The family with the maroon car lives on the floor immediately below the family with the olive car.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 5: “There is exactly one floor between the floors of the family of four and the family with the silver car.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 6: “There are exactly 2 floors between the floors of the Gills and the family with the silver car.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 7: “There is exactly one floor between the floors of the family of three and the Gills.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 8: “Either the Gills lives on floor 4 or the family with the silver car lives on floor 2, but not both.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 9: “Either the Joshis lives on floor 6 or the family with the maroon car lives on floor 5, but not both.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 10: “The family with the teal car lives on either the top floor or the bottom floor.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 11: “The Iyers lives on either the top floor or the bottom floor.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 12: “The family with the maroon car and the family of seven live on adjacent floors.” Now fixed: the Gills: maroon. Step 13: “The Chawlas lives on some floor below the family of two.” Now fixed: the Bhatts on floor 5; the Chawlas on floor 3; the Dases on floor 2; the Gills on floor 4; the Iyers on floor 1; the Joshis on floor 6; the Bhatts: olive; the Chawlas: navy; the Dases: beige; the Iyers: silver; the Joshis: teal; the Bhatts: seven; the Chawlas: four; the Dases: three; the Iyers: five; the Joshis: two. Every possible arrangement was checked by computer and exactly one fits all the clues: floor 1: the Iyers (silver, five); floor 2: the Dases (beige, three); floor 3: the Chawlas (navy, four); floor 4: the Gills (maroon, six); floor 5: the Bhatts (olive, seven); floor 6: the Joshis (teal, two). the Gills is in floor 4; the person on the floor immediately above is the Bhatts.
