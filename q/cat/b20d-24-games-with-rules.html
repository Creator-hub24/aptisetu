CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Games with rules · B20D-24

MCQ · Medium

Data set: Sealed-bid auction (Medium)
Sealed-bid auction

Four collectors, W, X, Y and Z, each start with 100 tokens. Five lots are auctioned one after another, Lot 1 to Lot 5. Before the auction each collector wrote down a planned bid for every lot (table below). The rules are:

  • For each lot, a collector's actual bid is the planned bid, or the tokens he has left at that moment if that is smaller.
  • The highest actual bid wins the lot, but the winner pays the second-highest actual bid for that lot.
  • If two collectors tie for the highest bid, the one with more tokens left wins (this never happens below).
CollectorLot 1Lot 2Lot 3Lot 4Lot 5
W4030352050
X3545104030
Y3040453025
Z4520304540
If instead the winner of each lot paid his own actual bid (everything else unchanged), how many tokens would Z have at the end?
A0
B10
C15
D20
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: B. 10

Solution: Lot 1: Z bids 45 (highest), second-highest is W's 40 — Z pays 40 and has 60 left. Lot 2: X 45 wins and pays Y's 40 (X left 60). Lot 3: Y 45 wins and pays W's 35 (Y left 65). Lot 4: Z 45 (Z still has 60) wins and pays X's 40 — Z has 20 left. Lot 5: Z's planned 40 is cut to 20 tokens; W 50 wins and pays the second-highest actual bid, X's 30 (W left 70). Final tokens: W 70, X 60, Y 65, Z 20. With pay-your-bid: Z wins Lot 1 for 45 (55 left), X wins Lot 2 for 45, Y wins Lot 3 for 45, Z wins Lot 4 for 45 (10 left), and W wins Lot 5 for 50 (Z's bid is cut to 10). Z ends with 10.
