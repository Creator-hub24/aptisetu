CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Scheduling · B20D-29

MCQ · Medium

Data set: Exam timetable (Medium)
Exam timetable

Six papers — Accounts, Biology, Chemistry, Drawing, Economics and French — are to be held over eight days, Day 1 to Day 8, at most one paper a day. So exactly two days have no paper (free days).

  • There is a paper on Day 1 and on Day 8, and the two free days are not next to each other.
  • Biology is held before Chemistry, and they are not on consecutive days.
  • Economics is held exactly three days after Accounts, and there is no free day between them.
  • French is held on the day just after a free day.
  • Biology is held on an even-numbered day.
  • Chemistry is held after French.
Which paper is held on Day 3?
ABiology
BFrench
CEconomics
DDrawing
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: D. Drawing

Solution: Accounts and Economics are 3 days apart with no free day between, so Accounts, two other papers and Economics fill four consecutive days. French follows a free day and Chemistry comes after French, so Chemistry is not on Day 1–3 and French is not on Day 1. Trying the placements (the computer checks all 20,160 ways): the only timetable that fits is Day 1 Accounts, Day 2 Biology, Day 3 Drawing, Day 4 Economics, Day 5 free, Day 6 French, Day 7 free, Day 8 Chemistry. Check: free days 5 and 7 not adjacent ✓; Biology (2) before Chemistry (8), not consecutive ✓; Economics 4 = Accounts 1 + 3 with Days 2–3 busy ✓; French on 6 after free Day 5 ✓; Biology on an even day ✓; Chemistry after French ✓.
