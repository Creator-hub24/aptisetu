CAT Quantitative Ability: Ratio & Proportion · B2Q-10

MCQ · Medium

A hostel's monthly cost is partly fixed and partly proportional to the number of students. With 40 students the cost per student is ₹3,000 a month; with 60 students it is ₹2,500. What is the cost per student with 100 students?
A₹1,800
B₹2,000
C₹2,100
D₹2,250
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: C. ₹2,100

Solution: Total cost = F + nv. 40 students: F + 40v = 1,20,000; 60 students: F + 60v = 1,50,000. So v = 1,500 and F = 60,000. For 100 students the total is 60,000 + 1,50,000 = 2,10,000, i.e. ₹2,100 per student.
