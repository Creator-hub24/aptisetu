CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Arrangements · Z1D-077

TITA · Hard

Data set: Desk row (Hard)
Desk row

Six engineers, Aakash, Bhoomi, Chandan, Deepika, Eklavya and Gargi, sit in a row of six desks numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. Each uses a different laptop brand, Arco, Brisk, Corvo, Delta, Echo and Fable; each codes mainly in a different language, Python, Java, Go, Rust, Kotlin and Swift; and each started on a different weekday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday.

The following is known:

  • Eklavya codes in Java.
  • There are exactly 3 people between the Fable user and Bhoomi.
  • There are exactly 3 people between the Corvo user and Gargi.
  • Deepika codes in Rust.
  • The Arco user is somewhere to the left of the person who codes in Java.
  • Eklavya is somewhere to the left of Bhoomi.
  • The person who started on Tuesday and the person who codes in Go are next to each other.
  • Deepika is immediately to the left of the person who codes in Kotlin.
  • There are exactly 3 people between Bhoomi and the person who codes in Python.
  • Either the Brisk user is in desk 4 or the person who started on Saturday is in desk 4, but not both.
  • Chandan and the Corvo user are next to each other.
  • Bhoomi is somewhere to the left of the person who started on Saturday.
  • The Delta user and the person who started on Friday are next to each other.
  • Either the person who started on Wednesday is in desk 4 or Eklavya is in desk 2, but not both.
  • The person who started on Tuesday is immediately to the left of the person who started on Friday.
  • The person who started on Saturday and the person who started on Monday are next to each other.
How many people are between Bhoomi and Gargi?

Type in the answer (TITA): no options.

Show answer & solution

Correct answer: 3

Solution: Step 1: “Eklavya codes in Java.” Now fixed: Eklavya: Java. Step 2: “Deepika codes in Rust.” Now fixed: Deepika: Rust. Step 3: “Deepika is immediately to the left of the person who codes in Kotlin.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 4: “The person who started on Tuesday is immediately to the left of the person who started on Friday.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 5: “There are exactly 3 people between the Fable user and Bhoomi.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 6: “There are exactly 3 people between the Corvo user and Gargi.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 7: “There are exactly 3 people between Bhoomi and the person who codes in Python.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 8: “Either the Brisk user is in desk 4 or the person who started on Saturday is in desk 4, but not both.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 9: “Either the person who started on Wednesday is in desk 4 or Eklavya is in desk 2, but not both.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 10: “The person who started on Tuesday and the person who codes in Go are next to each other.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 11: “Chandan and the Corvo user are next to each other.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 12: “The Delta user and the person who started on Friday are next to each other.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 13: “The person who started on Saturday and the person who started on Monday are next to each other.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 14: “The Arco user is somewhere to the left of the person who codes in Java.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 15: “Eklavya is somewhere to the left of Bhoomi.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 16: “Bhoomi is somewhere to the left of the person who started on Saturday.” Now fixed: Aakash in desk 2; Bhoomi in desk 5; Chandan in desk 6; Deepika in desk 4; Eklavya in desk 3; Gargi in desk 1; Aakash: Arco; Bhoomi: Corvo; Chandan: Echo; Deepika: Brisk; Eklavya: Delta; Gargi: Fable; Aakash: Go; Bhoomi: Kotlin; Chandan: Swift; Gargi: Python; Aakash: Friday; Bhoomi: Monday; Chandan: Saturday; Deepika: Wednesday; Eklavya: Thursday; Gargi: Tuesday. Every possible arrangement was checked by computer and exactly one fits all the clues: desk 1: Gargi (Fable, Python, Tuesday); desk 2: Aakash (Arco, Go, Friday); desk 3: Eklavya (Delta, Java, Thursday); desk 4: Deepika (Brisk, Rust, Wednesday); desk 5: Bhoomi (Corvo, Kotlin, Monday); desk 6: Chandan (Echo, Swift, Saturday). Bhoomi (Bhoomi) is in desk 5 and Gargi in desk 1, so 3 people are between them.
