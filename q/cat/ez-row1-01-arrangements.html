CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Arrangements · EZ-ROW1-01

MCQ · Easy

Data set: Arrangement (Easy)
Arrangement

Five friends, Arun, Bina, Chetan, Deepa and Esha, park their cars in a row of five bays numbered 1 to 5 from left to right, one car per bay. Each car is a different colour: red, blue, white, black and grey.

The following is known:

  • Bina's colour is white.
  • Deepa is immediately to the left of Arun.
  • Chetan's colour is red.
  • Arun is immediately to the left of Esha.
  • Bina is not in bay 1.
  • The colour in bay 2 is blue.
  • Arun's colour is grey.
Who is in bay 2?
ADeepa
BEsha
CArun
DChetan
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. Deepa

Solution: Step 1: use "Bina's colour is white." — this fixes: Bina's colour is white. Step 2: use "Chetan's colour is red." — this fixes: Chetan's colour is red. Step 3: use "Arun's colour is grey." — this fixes: Arun's colour is grey. Step 4: use "Deepa is immediately to the left of Arun." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 5: use "Arun is immediately to the left of Esha." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 6: use "The colour in bay 2 is blue." — this fixes: Deepa's colour is blue; Esha's colour is black; Arun is in bay 3; Deepa is in bay 2; Esha is in bay 4. Step 7: use "Bina is not in bay 1." — this fixes: Bina is in bay 5; Chetan is in bay 1. Final arrangement: bay 1: Chetan (red); bay 2: Deepa (blue); bay 3: Arun (grey); bay 4: Esha (black); bay 5: Bina (white). Reading off: Deepa.
