CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Tables with missing data · DP-TABLE14-63

TITA · Hard

Data set: Data tables (missing data) (Hard)
Data tables (missing data)

The table shows the number of solar pumps installed by four district offices in three quarters. Some entries are hidden. Every figure (hidden or shown) is a multiple of 5 between 10 and 80, and the grand total of all twelve figures is 600.

Q1Q2Q3
Nashik?8040
Satara7070?
Latur??10
Akola65??

The following is known:

  • The figure for Latur in Q2 exceeded that for Satara in Q3 by 10.
  • The four together recorded 220 pumps in Q1.
  • Nashik's total across the three columns was 180 pumps.
  • The four together recorded 130 pumps in Q3.
  • The figure for Latur in Q1 was greater than that for Latur in Q2.
What percentage of Akola's total came in Q3? (Answer to the nearest integer.)

Type in the answer (TITA): no options.

Show answer & solution

Correct answer: 33

Solution: Step 1: use "Nashik's total across the three columns was 180 pumps." — this fixes: Nashik–Q1 = 60. Step 2: use "The four together recorded 220 pumps in Q1." — this fixes: Latur–Q1 = 25. Step 3: use "The figure for Latur in Q2 exceeded that for Satara in Q3 by 10." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 4: use "The figure for Latur in Q1 was greater than that for Latur in Q2." — this fixes: Satara–Q3 = 10; Latur–Q2 = 20. Step 5: use "The four together recorded 130 pumps in Q3." — this fixes: Akola–Q3 = 70; Akola–Q2 = 80. Final arrangement: Akola–Q3 = 70; Satara–Q3 = 10; Nashik–Q1 = 60; Akola–Q2 = 80; Latur–Q2 = 20; Latur–Q1 = 25. Reading off: 33.
