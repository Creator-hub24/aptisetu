CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Arrangements · Z1D-017

MCQ · Easy

Data set: Starting lanes (Easy)
Starting lanes

Five runners, Aditi, Bhavesh, Charu, Dhruv and Elina, start a race from five lanes numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. Each wears a different bib number: 11, 22, 33, 44 and 55.

The following is known:

  • The runner wearing bib 55 is in lane 4.
  • The runner wearing bib 11 is in lane 5.
  • Dhruv and Bhavesh are next to each other.
  • Dhruv and Elina are next to each other.
  • Charu wears bib 55.
  • Elina wears bib 33.
  • Dhruv is immediately to the left of the runner wearing bib 22.
Which bib number does Aditi wear?
A11
B22
C33
D55
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. 11

Solution: Step 1: “The runner wearing bib 55 is in lane 4.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 2: “The runner wearing bib 11 is in lane 5.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 3: “Charu wears bib 55.” Now fixed: Charu in lane 4; Charu: 55. Step 4: “Elina wears bib 33.” Now fixed: Elina: 33. Step 5: “Dhruv is immediately to the left of the runner wearing bib 22.” Now fixed: Dhruv: 44. Step 6: “Dhruv and Bhavesh are next to each other.” Now fixed: Aditi in lane 5; Aditi: 11; Bhavesh: 22. Step 7: “Dhruv and Elina are next to each other.” Now fixed: Bhavesh in lane 3; Dhruv in lane 2; Elina in lane 1. Every possible arrangement was checked by computer and exactly one fits all the clues: lane 1: Elina (33); lane 2: Dhruv (44); lane 3: Bhavesh (22); lane 4: Charu (55); lane 5: Aditi (11). Reading off: Aditi — 11.
