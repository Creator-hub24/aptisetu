CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Distribution · DP-GROUP17-77

MCQ · Medium

Data set: Grouping & selection (Hard)
Grouping & selection

Seven trainee analysts are split into three project teams: Team Kestrel has three members, Team Heron and Team Ibis have two each. Exactly three of the seven are senior; the rest are junior. The seven are Manav, Ela, Harsh, Sana, Chirag, Nisha, Omkar.

The following is known:

  • Ela is not in Team Heron.
  • Omkar is junior.
  • Team Kestrel has exactly one senior member.
  • Ela is not in Team Ibis.
  • Ela and Sana are in different teams.
  • Ela and Harsh are in different teams.
  • Harsh and Omkar are in the same team.
  • Every teammate of Chirag is junior.
  • Team Ibis has no senior member.
Which team is Ela in?
ATeam Kestrel
BTeam Ibis
CCannot be determined
DTeam Heron
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: A. Team Kestrel

Solution: Step 1: use "Omkar is junior." — this fixes: Omkar is junior. Step 2: use "Harsh and Omkar are in the same team." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 3: use "Team Ibis has no senior member." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 4: use "Team Kestrel has exactly one senior member." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 5: use "Every teammate of Chirag is junior." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 6: use "Ela is not in Team Heron." — nothing is fixed outright yet, but it removes options needed later. Step 7: use "Ela is not in Team Ibis." — this fixes: Ela is in Team Kestrel. Step 8: use "Ela and Harsh are in different teams." — this fixes: Harsh is in Team Ibis; Chirag is in Team Kestrel; Omkar is in Team Ibis; Ela is junior; Harsh is junior; Chirag is senior. Step 9: use "Ela and Sana are in different teams." — this fixes: Sana is in Team Heron; Sana is senior. After all clues, 2 arrangements remain: Team Kestrel: Manav, Ela, Chirag (S); Team Heron: Sana (S), Nisha (S); Team Ibis: Harsh, Omkar | Team Kestrel: Ela, Chirag (S), Nisha; Team Heron: Manav (S), Sana (S); Team Ibis: Harsh, Omkar. The quantity asked has the same value in each, so it is determined. Reading off: Team Kestrel.
