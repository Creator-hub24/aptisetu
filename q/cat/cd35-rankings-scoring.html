Data set: Three-round quiz
Three-round quiz

Four contestants W, X, Y and Z played three rounds. In each round the contestants were ranked 1st to 4th (no ties) and earned 4, 3, 2 and 1 points respectively. The final totals were 10, 8, 7 and 5 points, in some order.

  • Y had the highest total.
  • X won Round 1 but came last in Round 3.
  • Z came last in Round 1, and finished ahead of W in Round 2.
  • Each round had a different winner.
  • W's total was higher than Z's.
Who came second in Round 2?
AW
BX
CY
DZ
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: B. X

Solution: R1 winner is X and Z is last. Each round has a different winner, so Y wins exactly one of R2/R3. Y = 10 = 4 + 3 + 3, so Y is 2nd in the other two rounds, giving R1: X, Y, W, Z. X = 4 + (R2 points) + 1 must be 8, 7 or 5, so X is 2nd (X = 8) or 3rd (X = 7) in R2. Case Y wins R3: R2's winner is Z (Z must be ahead of W) and Y is 2nd, so X is 3rd (X = 7) and W 4th. Then W and Z must total 8 and 5. Z = 1 + 4 + (2 or 3) ≥ 7, so Z = 8 and W = 5, contradicting W > Z. So Y wins R2. If R2 = Y, Z, X, W, then X = 7, and R3 (won by W or Z) makes either W = 7 (a repeat of X's 7) or W < Z. So R2 = Y, X, Z, W and X = 8. R3 = winner, Y, other, X. Z winning gives Z = 7 > W = 5 ✗, so W wins. Final: R1 X > Y > W > Z; R2 Y > X > Z > W; R3 W > Y > Z > X. Totals W 7, X 8, Y 10, Z 5.
