CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Arrangements · Z1D-108

MCQ · Hard

Data set: Hexagonal table (Hard)
Hexagonal table

Six analysts, Mahesh, Nirmala, Oindrila, Prakash, Rohini and Sameer, sit around a hexagonal table in seats numbered 1 to 6 clockwise, all facing the centre (the person on someone's immediate left is in the next seat clockwise). Each covers a different sector, banking, cement, pharma, steel, telecom and textiles, and each has a different rating, A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 and C2.

The following is known:

  • The textiles analyst sits next to the analyst rated C2.
  • The textiles analyst sits next to the pharma analyst.
  • Either the analyst rated B1 sits in seat 1 or the cement analyst sits in seat 5, but not both.
  • Sameer sits next to the cement analyst.
  • The analyst rated B1 sits next to Sameer.
  • The analyst rated A1 sits to the immediate left of the steel analyst.
  • The analyst rated C1 sits next to the telecom analyst.
  • The steel analyst sits next to the telecom analyst.
  • Either Prakash sits in seat 3 or the pharma analyst sits in seat 3, but not both.
  • The analyst rated C2 sits directly opposite the cement analyst.
  • Nirmala sits directly opposite the analyst rated A1.
  • Mahesh sits second to the left of the banking analyst.
  • Prakash sits to the immediate left of Sameer.
  • Oindrila is rated A2.
Which of the following statements is true?
APrakash covers telecom
BRohini is rated A2
COindrila covers banking
DRohini sits in seat 2
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: C. Oindrila covers banking

Solution: Step 1: “Oindrila is rated A2.” Now fixed: Oindrila: A2. Step 2: “The analyst rated A1 sits to the immediate left of the steel analyst.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 3: “Prakash sits to the immediate left of Sameer.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 4: “The analyst rated C2 sits directly opposite the cement analyst.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 5: “Nirmala sits directly opposite the analyst rated A1.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 6: “Mahesh sits second to the left of the banking analyst.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 7: “Either the analyst rated B1 sits in seat 1 or the cement analyst sits in seat 5, but not both.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 8: “Either Prakash sits in seat 3 or the pharma analyst sits in seat 3, but not both.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 9: “The textiles analyst sits next to the analyst rated C2.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 10: “The textiles analyst sits next to the pharma analyst.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 11: “Sameer sits next to the cement analyst.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 12: “The analyst rated B1 sits next to Sameer.” Now fixed: Prakash in seat 3; Sameer in seat 2. Step 13: “The analyst rated C1 sits next to the telecom analyst.” Now fixed: Oindrila in seat 4; Prakash: cement. Step 14: “The steel analyst sits next to the telecom analyst.” Now fixed: Mahesh in seat 6; Nirmala in seat 5; Rohini in seat 1; Mahesh: pharma; Nirmala: textiles; Oindrila: banking; Rohini: steel; Sameer: telecom; Mahesh: C2; Nirmala: B2; Prakash: C1; Rohini: B1; Sameer: A1. Every possible arrangement was checked by computer and exactly one fits all the clues: seat 1: Rohini (steel, B1); seat 2: Sameer (telecom, A1); seat 3: Prakash (cement, C1); seat 4: Oindrila (banking, A2); seat 5: Nirmala (textiles, B2); seat 6: Mahesh (pharma, C2). Checking each option against this arrangement, only “Oindrila covers banking” holds.
