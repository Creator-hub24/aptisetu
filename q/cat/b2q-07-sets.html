CAT Quantitative Ability: Sets · B2Q-07

MCQ · Hard

Of a group of 100 tourists, 72 have visited Goa, 65 have visited Jaipur and 58 have visited Kochi. What is the minimum possible number of tourists who have visited at least two of these three cities?
A43
B48
C50
D95
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: B. 48

Solution: Total visits = 72 + 65 + 58 = 195 among at most 100 tourists. With x₁, x₂, x₃ tourists who visited exactly one, two and three cities, x₁ + 2x₂ + 3x₃ = 195 and x₁ + x₂ + x₃ ≤ 100, so x₂ + 2x₃ ≥ 95. To make x₂ + x₃ small, make x₃ as large as possible: x₃ = 47 and x₂ = 1 gives 48, and nothing smaller works because x₂ + x₃ ≥ (x₂ + 2x₃)/2 ≥ 47.5. A valid split: 47 visited all three, 1 visited Goa and Jaipur only, and 24, 17 and 11 visited only Goa, only Jaipur and only Kochi (total 100).
