CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Arrangements · Z1D-128

MCQ · Hard

Data set: Craft fair (Hard)
Craft fair

Six stalls in a row at a fair are numbered 1 to 6 from left to right and are run by Madhav, Nupur, Oshin, Pankaj, Radhika and Shaurya, one each. Each stall sells a different craft, baskets, bangles, carpets, dolls, masks and puppets; each was booked on a different date, 1st, 3rd, 5th, 8th, 13th and 21st; and each stallholder comes from a different district, Kutch, Bastar, Kangra, Madhubani, Nalanda and Sivasagar.

The following is known:

  • There are exactly 3 people between Pankaj and Madhav.
  • The bangles seller is at one of the two ends.
  • Either the stall booked on the 13th is in stall 1 or the carpets seller is in stall 6, but not both.
  • The stall booked on the 1st is somewhere to the left of the bangles seller.
  • The carpets seller is immediately to the left of Shaurya.
  • The stallholder from Bastar is immediately to the left of the stallholder from Kutch.
  • Either Pankaj is in stall 6 or the stallholder from Kangra is in stall 2, but not both.
  • There are exactly 2 people between the stall booked on the 1st and Radhika.
  • The stall booked on the 5th and Madhav are next to each other.
  • The stallholder from Nalanda is somewhere to the left of Madhav.
  • The dolls seller is somewhere to the left of the stall booked on the 3rd.
  • Oshin is at one of the two ends.
  • Shaurya and the masks seller are next to each other.
  • Madhav and the puppets seller are next to each other.
  • There are exactly 3 people between the baskets seller and the stallholder from Sivasagar.
  • The stallholder from Kutch and Pankaj are next to each other.
  • The stallholder from Bastar is immediately to the left of the stall booked on the 21st.
Which of the following statements is true?
AShaurya booked on the 3rd
BRadhika is in stall 6
CRadhika booked on the 3rd
DMadhav is in stall 2
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: D. Madhav is in stall 2

Solution: Step 1: “The carpets seller is immediately to the left of Shaurya.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 2: “The stallholder from Bastar is immediately to the left of the stallholder from Kutch.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 3: “The stallholder from Bastar is immediately to the left of the stall booked on the 21st.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 4: “There are exactly 3 people between Pankaj and Madhav.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 5: “There are exactly 2 people between the stall booked on the 1st and Radhika.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 6: “There are exactly 3 people between the baskets seller and the stallholder from Sivasagar.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 7: “Either the stall booked on the 13th is in stall 1 or the carpets seller is in stall 6, but not both.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 8: “Either Pankaj is in stall 6 or the stallholder from Kangra is in stall 2, but not both.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 9: “The bangles seller is at one of the two ends.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 10: “Oshin is at one of the two ends.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 11: “The stall booked on the 5th and Madhav are next to each other.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 12: “Shaurya and the masks seller are next to each other.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 13: “Madhav and the puppets seller are next to each other.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 14: “The stallholder from Kutch and Pankaj are next to each other.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 15: “The stall booked on the 1st is somewhere to the left of the bangles seller.” Note it; it rules out some placements but fixes nothing on its own yet. Step 16: “The stallholder from Nalanda is somewhere to the left of Madhav.” Now fixed: Madhav in stall 2; Nupur in stall 3; Oshin in stall 1; Pankaj in stall 6; Radhika in stall 5; Shaurya in stall 4; Madhav: baskets; Nupur: carpets; Oshin: puppets; Pankaj: bangles; Radhika: masks; Shaurya: dolls; Madhav: 1st; Nupur: 5th; Oshin: 13th; Radhika: 21st; Madhav: Madhubani; Nupur: Kangra; Oshin: Nalanda; Pankaj: Sivasagar; Radhika: Kutch; Shaurya: Bastar. Step 17: “The dolls seller is somewhere to the left of the stall booked on the 3rd.” Now fixed: Pankaj: 3rd; Shaurya: 8th. Every possible arrangement was checked by computer and exactly one fits all the clues: stall 1: Oshin (puppets, 13th, Nalanda); stall 2: Madhav (baskets, 1st, Madhubani); stall 3: Nupur (carpets, 5th, Kangra); stall 4: Shaurya (dolls, 8th, Bastar); stall 5: Radhika (masks, 21st, Kutch); stall 6: Pankaj (bangles, 3rd, Sivasagar). Checking each option against this arrangement, only “Madhav is in stall 2” holds.
