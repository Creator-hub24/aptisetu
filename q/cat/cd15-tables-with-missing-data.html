CAT Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning: Tables with missing data · CD15

MCQ · Hard

Data set: Bookshop sales
Bookshop sales (units)

A bookshop's sales of four genres over three months are shown. Some entries are missing.

GenreJanFebMarTotal
Fiction40??150
Science?20?100
History20?30?
Comics?30?130
Total150140?470
  • Every entry in the table is a multiple of 10 between 10 and 90.
  • In January, Comics sold exactly twice as many units as Science.
By what percentage did Comics sales change from February to March?
A25% increase
B33.33% increase
C25% decrease
D40% increase
Show answer & solution

Correct answer: B. 33.33% increase

Solution: History total = 470 − 150 − 100 − 130 = 90 ⇒ History Feb = 40. Feb column: Fiction Feb = 140 − 20 − 40 − 30 = 50 ⇒ Fiction Mar = 60. January: Science + Comics = 150 − 40 − 20 = 90 with Comics = 2 × Science ⇒ Science Jan = 30, Comics Jan = 60. Then Science Mar = 50 and Comics Mar = 40. Comics: 30 → 40, an increase of 10/30 = 33.33%.
