About AptiSetu

A free practice site for Indian students preparing for aptitude tests.

AptiSetu helps students practise Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, DILR and VARC for competitive exams and campus placements. It is free to use. Login only to save progress and unlock levels.

What's on the site

  • Bank / SSC / Placements track: Quant, Reasoning and DILR pages with concept notes, formula sheets and timed quizzes.
  • CAT track: VARC, DILR and QA with 40-minute sectionals, MCQ and TITA questions and CAT marking.
  • CUET (UG) track: General Aptitude Test, English and Mathematics with +5 / −1 marking at the real pace.

Our questions

Every question and passage on the site was written by our team. None are taken from past papers or any coaching material. Each comes with a worked solution, and our build process checks the answers automatically before anything is published. If you still spot a mistake, please tell us; see our corrections & editorial policy.

Who makes it

AptiSetu is made by Vaibhav Gupta, who has taught CAT and CLAT aptitude for six years, at Career Launcher and at Edumaths360 in Prayagraj. Questions and solutions are written and checked by our team; see how we fix errors on the corrections page. Write to edumaths360@gmail.com.

Independent

AptiSetu is an independent site. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the National Testing Agency (NTA), the IIMs, IBPS, SBI, SSC or any other exam body or company. Exam names are used only to describe what the practice is for.
