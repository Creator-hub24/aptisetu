Concept note

Syllogisms: use Venn diagrams

Draw the smallest diagram that fits every statement. A conclusion follows only if it is true in every possible diagram.

  • All A are B: circle A sits inside circle B. It also implies Some B are A.
  • Some A are B: the circles overlap. It implies Some B are A.
  • No A is B: the circles don't touch. It implies No B is A.
💡 "Some A are not B" never follows from "Some A are B" on its own. Look for a statement that rules it out.

Seating arrangement

Fix the most definite clue first, such as "X is in the middle" or "Y is at an end". In a row facing north, the person's right is your right. In a circle facing the centre, left is clockwise.

Blood relations

Draw a family tree. Use + for male and − for female, horizontal lines for siblings and spouses, and vertical lines for generations. Break long chains like "my father's father's only son" from the end backwards.

Formula & rules sheet

Syllogism: All A→B ⇒ Some B→A (conversion)
Some A→B ⇒ Some B→A ; No A→B ⇒ No B→A
Coding: A=1 … Z=26 ; reverse position = 27 − n
EJOTY → 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 (position anchors)
Clock angle = |30H − 5.5M|°
Hands overlap every 65 5/11 minutes
Odd days: normal year = 1, leap year = 2
Direction: right turn from N → E → S → W
Distance after turns: use Pythagoras √(x² + y²)
Series checks: differences, ratios, squares/cubes, n(n+1), ×2±1
