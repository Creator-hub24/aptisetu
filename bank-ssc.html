Exam pages

IBPS PO

Prelims → Mains (objective + descriptive) → Interview. Prelims is a qualifying round; its marks are not used in the final merit.

IBPS Clerk

Online Prelims → online Mains.

SBI PO

Phase I (Prelims) → Phase II (Mains) → Phase III (psychometric test, group exercise, interview). Final merit adds Phase II and Phase III marks; Phase I marks are not added.

SSC CGL

Two computer-based tiers. Tier I is for shortlisting; Tier II Paper I is compulsory for all posts.

SSC CHSL

Two computer-based tiers, plus a skill/typing test inside Tier II.
