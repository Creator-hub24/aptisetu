Concept note

Percentages: the core idea

A percentage is a fraction with denominator 100. Learn the fraction–percentage pairs (1/8 = 12.5%, 1/6 ≈ 16.67%, 1/12 ≈ 8.33%) so you can do percentage maths mentally.

Successive change: if a value changes by a% and then by b%, the net change is a + b + ab/100%. For example, +20% then −20% gives 20 − 20 − 4 = −4%.

💡 Shortcut: after a fall of x%, the rise needed to get back to the original value is x/(100 − x) × 100%. After a 20% fall you need a 25% rise.

Profit & Loss

Profit % and loss % are always calculated on cost price. Discount is always calculated on marked price. Selling at a loss of x% means SP = CP × (100 − x)/100.

Time, Speed & Distance

To convert km/h to m/s, multiply by 5/18. A train crossing a pole covers its own length. A train crossing a platform covers its own length plus the platform's. For equal distances at speeds x and y, the average speed is the harmonic mean 2xy/(x + y), not the simple average.

Speed = Distance / Time ; 1 km/h = 5/18 m/s
Avg speed (equal distances) = 2xy/(x + y)
Work: if A takes a days and B takes b days, together = ab/(a + b)
Trailing zeros of n! = ⌊n/5⌋ + ⌊n/25⌋ + ⌊n/125⌋ + …

x² + 1/x² = (x + 1/x)² − 2
nPr = n!/(n − r)! ; nCr = n!/(r!(n − r)!)
Arrangements with repeats = n!/(p! q! …)
P(E) = favourable / total outcomes
