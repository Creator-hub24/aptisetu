Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you use AptiSetu. In short: you can use most of the site without giving us anything. If you log in, we store your email address, your test and set results and the optional profile details you choose to add (name, city, state, exam). We don't sell your data.

Login (optional)

Login is only needed to save your progress and unlock levels in the CAT practice sets, keep your test history across devices, and save your Open Mock scores. When you log in, we use Supabase (our login and database provider) to send you a one-time login link and to store:

  • your email address (to log you in),
  • your results: CAT practice-set results (section, set number, score, total and the time you finished), your saved test history and your Open Mock scores, and
  • the details listed in the account section below, which are all optional.

We use this to show your progress and unlock levels, to understand which exams and regions students prepare from, and to improve the site. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers or use it for marketing. To have your account and results deleted, email edumaths360@gmail.com from the address you log in with. Supabase keeps a login session in your browser's local storage while you are logged in; logging out removes it.

Your quiz answers and progress

Quizzes run in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us, with one exception: when you are logged in and submit an Open Mock section, your answers for that section are sent to our server so it can score them (see below). Otherwise, when you are logged in, only the results listed in this policy (for example the final score of a CAT practice set or a saved test) are sent.

To let you resume an unfinished quiz and see your daily streak and topic-wise accuracy, the site saves this information in your browser's local storage on your device. It contains only quiz data (questions in progress, your answers, dates you practised and accuracy counts), never your name or contact details, and it is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time with the Reset my progress button on the Practice page, or by clearing this site's data (cookies and site data) in your browser settings.

The backup code on the Login page is made in your browser from this local data so you can move it to another device. It is not sent to us. The Quant Lab practice page likewise saves your answered and correct counts, best score and topic-wise accuracy in local storage on your device. This is never sent to us or anyone else. Its Reset stats button, or clearing site data, deletes it.

Cookies

The site does not set cookies. Local storage (above) is kept only on your device.

Third-party services

  • Login and saved results: handled by Supabase (supabase.com) when you log in. See Supabase's privacy policy for details.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Email: if you email us (including through a "Report an error" link, which opens your own email app with the question ID filled in), we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, open-source analytics service, to count visits. It does not set cookies and does not collect personal information. We see only aggregate numbers: which pages are viewed, the referring website, campaign tags in links (utm_ parameters), and general browser, screen-size, country and (for visitors in India) state statistics.

We also count a few named events, such as a quiz or sectional being started, finished, or submitted automatically when time runs out (for example sectional-start-cat-qa). These events record only the event name, not your answers, scores or anything about you. See GoatCounter's privacy policy for how it handles data.

What we store when you have an account

This applies only if you log in. The data is stored in our Supabase project (database hosted in India, Mumbai region). Only the site owner can open it, in Supabase's admin dashboard. Other students can never see it.

  • Account: your email address, and when you signed up and last logged in. Supabase's login service may also log technical details such as your IP address for security.
  • Profile (optional): your name, and your city, state and the exam you are preparing for. You can leave any of these empty and change them on My account.
  • Saved history: the test name, score, maximum, accuracy, number of questions and time taken of each finished test, and your CAT practice-set results.
  • Open Mock: when you submit a section, your answers for it are sent to our server to be scored. We store the score, number correct and wrong, and the time. The Open Mock is currently unranked practice: there is no public leaderboard, and other students cannot see your scores.
  • Why: to show you your progress and saved scores, to support you if you write to us, and to see in total which exams and regions students prepare from so we know what to build next.

Keeping and deleting: we keep this while your account exists. To have your account and all of this data deleted, email edumaths360@gmail.com from the address you log in with and we will do it. You can also ask us what we hold about you, or to correct it.

Children

The site is meant for students, including school students preparing for CUET (UG). You can use all the practice without an account and without giving us any personal information. If you are under 18, please create an account only with the permission of a parent or guardian.
