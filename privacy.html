Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you use AptiSetu. In short: you can use most of the site without giving us anything. If you log in, we store your email address and your CAT practice-set results, and nothing else.

Login (optional)

Login is only needed to save progress and unlock levels in the CAT practice sets. When you log in, we use Supabase (our login and database provider) to send you a one-time login link and to store:

  • your email address (to log you in), and
  • your set results: the section, set number, score, total and the time you finished.

We use this only to show your progress and unlock levels. We don't sell it or use it for marketing. To have your account and results deleted, email edumaths360@gmail.com from the address you log in with. Supabase keeps a login session in your browser's local storage while you are logged in; logging out removes it.

Your quiz answers and progress

Quizzes run in your browser. Your answers are never sent to us. The only thing sent is the final score of a CAT practice set, and only when you are logged in (see above).

To let you resume an unfinished quiz and see your daily streak and topic-wise accuracy, the site saves this information in your browser's local storage on your device. It contains only quiz data (questions in progress, your answers, dates you practised and accuracy counts), never your name or contact details, and it is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time with the Reset my progress button on the Practice page, or by clearing this site's data (cookies and site data) in your browser settings.

Cookies

The site does not set cookies. Local storage (above) is kept only on your device.

Third-party services

  • Login and saved results: handled by Supabase (supabase.com) when you log in. See Supabase's privacy policy for details.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. See Google's privacy policy for details.
  • Email: if you email us (including through a "Report an error" link, which opens your own email app with the question ID filled in), we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

Analytics

We don't use analytics today. If we add visitor counts in future, we will use a privacy-friendly tool that does not use cookies or collect personal data, and we will update this page first.

Children

The site is meant for students, including school students preparing for CUET (UG). Because we don't collect personal information, no information about children is collected.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if anything changes. Questions? Contact us.
