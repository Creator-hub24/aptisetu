IBPS Clerk (CRP CSA-XVI): key facts

Pattern
Online Prelims → online Mains.
Prelims
English 30 Q / 30 marks · Numerical Ability 35 / 35 · Reasoning Ability 35 / 35. 100 marks, 60 minutes (20 minutes per section); you must qualify in each test.
Mains
General/Financial Awareness 40 Q / 50 marks / 20 min · General English 40 / 40 / 35 min · Reasoning & Computer Aptitude 40 / 60 / 35 min · Quantitative Aptitude 40 / 50 / 35 min. 160 Q, 200 marks, 125 minutes.
Marking
Wrong answers cost ¼ of the marks for that question.

Source: IBPS CRP CSA-XVI (Clerk) notification · ibps.in (official IBPS site). Checked 27 Oct 2026. Conducted by IBPS; we are not affiliated with them. Always confirm on the official site.
