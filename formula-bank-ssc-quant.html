Percentages

  • Net change of a% then b% = a + b + ab/100 % Use negative values for falls.
  • Rise to recover a fall of x% = x/(100 − x) × 100 %
  • Price ↑ r% ⇒ cut use by r/(100 + r) × 100 % Same spending.

Profit, loss & discount

  • Profit % = (SP − CP)/CP × 100 Always on cost price.
  • SP = MP × (1 − d/100) Discount is on marked price.
  • Successive discounts d₁, d₂ = d₁ + d₂ − d₁d₂/100 %
  • Same SP, x% gain and x% loss ⇒ loss of x²/100 %

Interest

  • SI = PRT/100
  • A = P(1 + R/100)ⁿ
  • CI − SI (2 years) = P(R/100)²
  • CI − SI (3 years) = P(R/100)²(3 + R/100)
  • Half-yearly: rate R/2, periods 2n

Speed & work

  • km/h × 5/18 = m/s
  • Average speed (equal distances) = 2xy/(x + y)
  • Boat b = (down + up)/2, stream s = (down − up)/2
  • A alone a days, B alone b days ⇒ together ab/(a + b) days
  • M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂ Men × days × hours per unit of work.

Averages & mixtures

  • Average = sum/count
  • Alligation: cheaper : dearer = (d − m) : (m − c)
  • After n replacements of fraction f: left = (1 − f)ⁿ of original

Mensuration

  • Rectangle area lw, perimeter 2(l + w)
  • Trapezium area = ½(a + b)h
  • Rhombus area = ½d₁d₂
  • Cube V = a³, S = 6a²; cuboid S = 2(lb + bh + hl)
  • Cylinder CSA = 2πrh, TSA = 2πr(r + h)
  • Cone slant l = √(r² + h²), CSA = πrl
