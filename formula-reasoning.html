Letters & coding

  • A–Z positions: E = 5, J = 10, O = 15, T = 20, Y = 25 Remember 'EJOTY'.
  • Opposite letter: position of opposite = 27 − position A ↔ Z, M ↔ N.
  • Reverse position = 27 − forward position

Clocks

  • Angle between hands = |30H − 5.5M|° Take 360 minus it if over 180.
  • Hands coincide every 720/11 = 65 5/11 minutes
  • Hands overlap 22 times, are opposite 22 times, and at right angles 44 times in 24 hours

Calendars

  • Odd days: ordinary year 1, leap year 2
  • Leap year: divisible by 4; century years only if divisible by 400 1900 no, 2000 yes.
  • Days of the week repeat every 7 days; 100 years have 5 odd days

Ranking & order

  • Total = rank from left + rank from right − 1
  • People between two ranks (same side) = difference − 1

Directions

  • Turning right from North gives East; left gives West
  • Shortest distance = √(x² + y²) Net east-west x, net north-south y.

Inequalities & syllogisms

  • A ≥ B > C ⇒ A > C; A ≥ B ≤ C ⇒ no relation
  • Either-or: when two conclusions are complementary and neither alone follows
  • All A are B + All B are C ⇒ All A are C
  • Some A are B ⇒ Some B are A; 'All A are B' ⇒ 'Some B are A' Possibility questions: check if it can be true.
