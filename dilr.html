Concept note

How to approach a DI set

  1. Scan for 30 seconds: note the units, the totals and what each axis or column means.
  2. Approximate: most DI options are far enough apart that rounding works (e.g. 487/1210 ≈ 40%).
  3. Reuse: compute totals or growth once and use them for every question in the set.

Growth and share

Growth % = (New − Old)/Old × 100. Share % = Part/Total × 100. Don't mix these up. A product can have the highest absolute increase but not the highest percentage growth.

💡 Pie charts: 1% = 3.6°, so 25% = 90° and 10% = 36°.

Choosing LR sets (CAT)

In CAT, set selection matters as much as solving. Spend the first 3–4 minutes scanning every set, then start with the ones that have the most definite clues and the fewest variables.

⭕Venn Diagrams5
  • n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B)
  • n(A ∪ B ∪ C) = ΣA − Σ(pairs) + n(A ∩ B ∩ C)Here 'pairs' includes those in all three.
  • Only A = A − (A∩B) − (A∩C) + (A∩B∩C)
  • Exactly two = Σ(pairs) − 3 × n(A ∩ B ∩ C)
  • None = Total − n(at least one)
🏅Games & Tournaments3
  • Round robin (n teams) = nC2 = n(n − 1) / 2 matchesEach pair of teams plays once.
  • Knock-out (n teams) = n − 1 matchesEach match eliminates one team.
  • Total points = points per match × matchesE.g. 2 per win with no draws: 2 × matches.
