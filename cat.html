⏱ Sectional mode

  • One section, 40 minutes, submits automatically when time runs out.
  • About 24 VARC, 22 DILR or 22 QA questions, picked from the bank (DILR and RC sets stay together).
  • A CAT-style palette: not visited, not answered, answered, marked, and answered & marked.
  • RC passages and DILR sets open in a side panel on desktop and above the question on mobile.
  • Results: score, attempts, accuracy, time per question and a topic-wise strong/weak breakdown.

🧠 Practice mode

  • Pick a section and a topic; no countdown (just an elapsed clock).
  • Check each answer and see the full solution straight away.
  • Useful for learning one question type at a time before taking sectionals.
